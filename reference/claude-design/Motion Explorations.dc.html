The Proof Is in How It Moves.

  1. State 1. The title sits on the reel, which is blurred 40px under a 70% white veil, so it could be a gradient or a video. The heading's idle glitch keeps running.
  2. State 2. The first scroll is held while the title glitches out word by word (repo engine, 3 → 36px blocks) and the veil and blur clear over 900ms. After that the page scrolls normally, and the reel shrinks from full-bleed into the hero's section padding (62px nav height on top, 22px gutters, 12px corners, from Hero.astro) as the next section comes up. Back at the top, scrolling up anywhere within the hero glides back to the top and reverses it: the title resolves in and the veil returns.
