Turn 6 · Gradient system proposal

Soft-wash gradients

The current vertical peach-to-sky gradient replaced by five two-tone washes. Each wash is two soft radial glows pinned to the top corners that fade into cool white by about 45% of the height. Surfaces move from white to stone (#eef3f6 on #f6f9fb) so the washes have something to melt into.
